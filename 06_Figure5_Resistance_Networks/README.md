# Figure 5 — Acquired resistance to KRAS G12C inhibitors and matching strategies

A schematic drawn with matplotlib (`code/run_figure5.py` → `figures/Fig5.{pdf,png,tif}`). Centre: RTK–SHP2–SOS1–KRAS–RAF–MEK–ERK pathway with the PI3K–AKT–mTOR branch. Right: resistance alterations reported in 38 patients treated with adagrasib (Awad et al., *N Engl J Med* 2021) — MET amplification and oncogenic fusions; switch-II pocket mutations (R68S, H95D/Q/R, Y96C), second-site activating mutations and *KRAS* G12C amplification; NRAS, BRAF and MAP2K1 mutations, BRAF/RAF1 fusions, NF1 and PTEN loss of function; squamous transformation. Adaptive EGFR feedback in colorectal cancer is from Amodio et al. (*Cancer Discov* 2020). Left: strategies discussed in Section 5.3 of the manuscript.
