# Key clinical takeaways / 临床要点（中英对照）

All figures are from the manuscript (data cut-off 18 November 2026). 数据均取自稿件，截止 2026 年 11 月 18 日。

1. **Pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC / 经治 KRAS G12C 非小细胞肺癌**
   - **EN:** Sotorasib and adagrasib improved PFS over docetaxel (HR 0.66 and 0.58). Sotorasib did not improve OS (HR 1.01; 26% of the docetaxel arm crossed over to sotorasib); adagrasib OS was immature at the primary report. Divarasib improved both PFS (HR 0.58) and OS (HR 0.56, interim) over sotorasib or adagrasib in Krascendo 1.
   - **CN：** sotorasib 与 adagrasib 较多西他赛延长 PFS（HR 0.66、0.58）。sotorasib 未改善 OS（HR 1.01；多西他赛组 26% 交叉至 sotorasib）；adagrasib 的 OS 在首次报告时尚不成熟。Krascendo 1 中 divarasib 较 sotorasib 或 adagrasib 同时延长 PFS（HR 0.58）与 OS（HR 0.56，期中分析）。

2. **Colorectal cancer / 结直肠癌**
   - **EN:** KRAS G12C inhibitor monotherapy has limited activity (response about 10%–20%) because of EGFR feedback. Sotorasib plus panitumumab improved PFS in chemorefractory disease (CodeBreaK 300). Adagrasib plus cetuximab did not improve PFS or OS over second-line chemotherapy (KRYSTAL-10), and this US indication was withdrawn at the sponsor's request on 1 September 2026.
   - **CN：** 由于 EGFR 反馈，KRAS G12C 抑制剂单药疗效有限（缓解率约 10%–20%）。sotorasib 联合帕尼单抗在化疗难治人群中延长 PFS（CodeBreaK 300）；adagrasib 联合西妥昔单抗在二线未优于化疗（KRYSTAL-10），该美国适应证已于 2026 年 9 月 1 日应申办方请求撤回。

3. **Pancreatic cancer / 胰腺癌**
   - **EN:** Daraxonrasib, a multi-RAS(ON) inhibitor, improved OS over chemotherapy in previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer (median 13.2 vs 6.7 months; HR 0.40, interim; crossover not permitted).
   - **CN：** multi-RAS(ON) 抑制剂 daraxonrasib 在经治转移性胰腺癌中较化疗延长 OS（中位 13.2 vs 6.7 个月，HR 0.40，期中分析；不允许交叉）。

4. **Co-mutations and toxicity / 共突变与毒性**
   - **EN:** *STK11* and *KEAP1* co-mutations are associated with poorer outcomes. Severe sotorasib hepatotoxicity is more frequent when sotorasib closely follows anti-PD-(L)1 therapy (33% vs 11% in one cohort); one study suggests not starting sotorasib within 30 days of the last anti-PD-(L)1 infusion.
   - **CN：** *STK11*、*KEAP1* 共突变与较差结局相关。sotorasib 紧接抗 PD-(L)1 治疗使用时严重肝毒性更常见（某队列 33% vs 11%），有研究建议末次抗 PD-(L)1 输注后 30 天内不启动 sotorasib。

5. **Resistance and ctDNA / 耐药与 ctDNA**
   - **EN:** After adagrasib, 17 of 38 patients had a putative resistance mechanism (secondary KRAS mutations, RTK/MAPK bypass, histological transformation). Early clearance of KRAS G12C ctDNA on sotorasib was associated with response (80% vs 8%) in a 32-patient cohort; whether ctDNA-guided treatment changes improve outcomes is untested.
   - **CN：** adagrasib 耐药后 38 例中 17 例检出可能机制（KRAS 二次突变、RTK/MAPK 旁路、组织学转化）。32 例 sotorasib 队列中 KRAS G12C ctDNA 早期清除与缓解相关（80% vs 8%）；依据 ctDNA 调整治疗能否改善结局尚无前瞻证据。
