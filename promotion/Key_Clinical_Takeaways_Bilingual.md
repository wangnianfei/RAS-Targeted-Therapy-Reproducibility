# Key Clinical Takeaways: "RAS-Targeted Therapy in Solid Tumours" (Bilingual)
# RAS靶向治疗关键临床结论速览（中英双语卡片）

---

### Takeaway 1 | Endpoint Discordance in 2L NSCLC
- **EN:** In 2L+ *KRAS* G12C NSCLC, direct inhibitor monotherapies consistently improve progression-free survival (PFS HR 0.58–0.66) but yield flat overall survival (OS HR 0.98–1.01). Crossover meta-regression confirms this is primarily driven by 30%–35% control-arm post-progression salvage targeted therapy, which prolongs control survival and dilutes observed OS HR toward unity.
- **CN（肺癌二线终点分离）：** 在经治 *KRAS* G12C 晚期非小细胞肺癌中，单药直接抑制剂显著延长 PFS（HR 0.58–0.66），但 OS 风险比趋近于 1.0（HR 0.98–1.01）。元回归分析证实这主要由对照组进展后 30%–35% 交叉接受靶向药物引起，后线治疗延长了对照组生存，导致统计学上的 OS 获益稀释。

---

### Takeaway 2 | Regulatory Fate of Chemo-Free Doublets in CRC
- **EN:** While vertical EGFR plus KRAS co-blockade is established in third-line refractory colorectal cancer (CodeBreaK 300), the phase III KRYSTAL-10 trial failed to confirm superiority of adagrasib plus cetuximab over standard doublet chemotherapy plus bevacizumab in second-line mCRC (BICR PFS HR 0.89, OS HR 0.94), resulting in the formal FDA withdrawal of accelerated approval on September 1, 2026. Chemo-free doublets cannot replace chemotherapy backbones in 2L CRC due to rapid polyclonal bypass emergence.
- **CN（结直肠癌二线免化疗方案的监管反思）：** 垂直双靶（EGFR + KRAS）虽已确立为结直肠癌三线标准方案（CodeBreaK 300），但在二线 KRYSTAL-10 确证性试验中，阿达格拉西布联合西妥昔单抗未能战胜化疗联合贝伐珠单抗（BICR PFS HR 0.89，OS HR 0.94），导致 FDA 于 2026 年 9 月正式撤销其加速批准。结直肠癌二线不能轻易抛弃化疗基石。

---

### Takeaway 3 | Pancreatic Cancer Breakthrough (RASolute 302)
- **EN:** In metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), the phase III RASolute 302 trial demonstrated that daraxonrasib (RMC-6236, a first-in-class multi-RAS(ON) tri-complex inhibitor) doubled median overall survival compared to standard chemotherapy (13.2 vs 6.7 months; OS HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.31–0.52, p < 0.0001). In the absence of effective post-progression targeted crossover in PDAC, PFS gains translated cleanly into unprecedented survival improvements, establishing multi-RAS inhibition as a standard of care.
- **CN（胰腺癌靶向治疗的历史性突破）：** 在晚期胰腺癌 III 期 RASolute 302 试验中，多靶点 RAS(ON) 三元复合物抑制剂达拉索拉西布（RMC-6236）较化疗使中位总生存期实现了翻倍（13.2 对 6.7 个月；OS HR 0.40，P < 0.0001）。由于胰腺癌缺乏后线靶向挽救治疗，几乎无交叉用药干扰，PFS 的延长直接转化为了显著的生存倍增。

### Takeaway 4 | Preemptive Interventional Window via Serial ctDNA
- **EN:** Circulating tumour DNA (ctDNA) kinetics represent an actionable molecular biomarker: complete clearance of mutant *KRAS* at Cycle 2 Day 1 strongly predicts durable disease control (ORR ~80% vs <10% in non-clearers; HR < 0.35). Furthermore, longitudinal ultra-sensitive ctDNA sequencing identifies emerging secondary resistance mutations (Y96, H95, MET amp, BRAF V600E) with a median lead time of 2 to 4 months prior to radiographic RECIST progression.
- **CN（外周血 ctDNA 提供的预警与干预窗口）：** 液体活检动态监测具有重要临床指导价值：第 2 周期第 1 天（C2D1）实现突变型 *KRAS* ctDNA 完全清除的患者客观缓解率达 80%（未清除者不足 10%，死亡风险降低 >65%）。同时，连续 ctDNA 测序较影像学进展提前 2 至 4 个月捕捉到继发耐药突变，为临床在宏观进展前调整联合策略提供了宝贵窗口。

---

### Takeaway 5 | Pipeline Transition: Direct vs Indirect & Combinations
- **EN:** Analysis of 1,182 active trials reveals a major pipeline transition: rational combination regimens now account for >60% of all ongoing clinical development. Direct multi-RAS(ON) and pan-KRAS approaches are rapidly eclipsing indirect node inhibitors (SOS1/SHP2), while next-generation allele-specific inhibitors (G12D, G12V) are driving global expansion, led by North America in novelty and East Asia in clinical delivery.
- **CN（全球管线转型：从单药到联合，从间接到直接）：** 1,182 项临床试验分析显示，联合用药方案占比已达 60% 以上；直接靶向泛 RAS/多 RAS(ON) 抑制剂的强劲疗效正在弱化对间接节点（SOS1/SHP2）单药的研发热情；G12D 等新型等位基因特异性抑制剂呈现爆发式增长，形成了北美引领源头创新、东亚主导快速转化的全球格局。
