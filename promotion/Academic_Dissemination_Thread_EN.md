**1/8** RAS was called undruggable for four decades. Our review in @ESMOOpen asks what seven phase III trials of RAS-pathway inhibitors have actually shown — and what they have not. Data and code are open. [DOI] #OncTwitter #NSCLC #CRCSM #PancreaticCancer

**2/8** Two drug classes now reach patients: covalent inhibitors that trap inactive KRAS G12C in the switch-II pocket (sotorasib, adagrasib, divarasib) and tri-complex inhibitors that bind active RAS together with cyclophilin A (daraxonrasib). [Fig 1: PDB 6OIM and 9BG6]

**3/8** Response rates improved in all seven trials and PFS in six. Overall survival is the harder test: it improved with divarasib vs sotorasib/adagrasib in pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC (Krascendo 1, HR 0.56, interim) and with daraxonrasib vs chemotherapy in pancreatic cancer (RASolute 302, median 13.2 vs 6.7 mo, HR 0.40, interim).

**4/8** Sotorasib did not improve OS over docetaxel (CodeBreaK 200, HR 1.01); 26% of the docetaxel arm crossed over to sotorasib, which complicates the comparison. OS for adagrasib in KRYSTAL-12 was immature at the primary report.

**5/8** Colorectal cancer needs EGFR co-blockade. Sotorasib + panitumumab improved PFS in refractory disease (CodeBreaK 300), but adagrasib + cetuximab did not beat second-line chemotherapy on PFS or OS (KRYSTAL-10), and that US indication was withdrawn at the sponsor's request on 1 Sep 2026. [Fig 3]

**6/8** Of 1,151 RAS-related interventional oncology trials on ClinicalTrials.gov (after record-level review), 353 test a directly RAS-targeted agent in solid tumours: 201 involve KRAS G12C inhibitors, 60 G12D inhibitors, 40 vaccines/TCR-T, 39 multi-RAS(ON) or pan-RAS inhibitors. [Suppl. Fig S2]

**7/8** Resistance is diverse. After adagrasib, 17/38 patients had a putative mechanism: secondary KRAS mutations, RTK and MAPK bypass, and squamous transformation (2 of 10 patients with a repeat tissue biopsy). [Fig 4]

**8/8** Next steps: inhibitors for non-G12C alleles, first-line and perioperative trials, combinations matched to resistance, and trials that pre-specify how crossover will be handled. Every data figure can be regenerated from our repository (Fig 4 is a schematic). #OpenScience

## LinkedIn post (template)

Our review of direct RAS inhibition in solid tumours has been accepted in *ESMO Open* [DOI]. We compare seven phase III trials and separate what they establish from what remains open: response rates and PFS improved broadly, whereas overall survival improved in two trials — divarasib versus first-generation G12C inhibitors in NSCLC and daraxonrasib versus chemotherapy in pancreatic cancer. We also classify 1,151 registered trials and summarise documented resistance mechanisms. Data and code for every figure: https://github.com/wangnianfei/RAS-Targeted-Therapy-Reproducibility
