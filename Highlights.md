# Highlights

**Manuscript Title:** Targeting RAS in solid tumours: from molecular switch dynamics and resistance networks to randomised clinical trial landscapes

- Direct RAS targeting now spans switch-II pockets and active multi-RAS glues.
- Co-mutations in STK11 and KEAP1 govern intrinsic resistance to RAS blockade.
- Post-progression crossover decouples PFS from OS in targeted phase 3 trials.
- Acquired resistance involves RTK rebound, switch-II mutations, and bypass loops.
- Vertical co-inhibition and ctDNA dynamics guide adaptive combination therapy.
